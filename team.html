Meet Your Leadership Team

You have an entire support system during your time as an intern. Leadership works across different levels and areas to support your development, your team, and the overall Peer Navigator program.

Lynn Kelly

Senior Manager

Lynn focuses on long-term goals, strategy, and big decisions that move our entire department forward. She guides the managers with team priorities, policies, and projects to make sure we are aligned with the vision of the University and fulfilling the commitments to our department. She supports the Director of Academic Services.

Academic Services includes the Peer Navigator program, Academic Coaching, Orientation, Student Communities, and Social Emotional Learning.

Managers

Managers are your day-to-day leaders. They guide Supervisors, support interns with progress and expectations, and help carry team priorities, projects, and quality standards into day-to-day work.

Supervisors

Supervisors lead specialized areas of the Peer Navigator program. Each supervisor owns a distinct part of the intern and student experience while partnering across the team to keep training, development, engagement, events, and operations aligned.

Jen Crain

Onboarding Supervisor

Jen leads the onboarding experience for all new interns, helping them build confidence and foundational coaching skills from day one. She oversees new hire training, specialized domain learning, and early development to ensure interns are prepared for success before transitioning to their ongoing leader.

Breanna Evenson

Continuing Education Supervisor

Bre partners with leaders to support ongoing intern growth through skill development, coaching quality, and targeted learning opportunities. She focuses on helping interns strengthen their coaching practice, deepen AFFIRM proficiency, and continue growing throughout their internship journey.

Randal Smith

Operations & Events Supervisor

Randal leads live event operations and process improvement efforts to create consistent, high-quality student experiences. He oversees event coordination, operational efficiencies, and cross-functional collaboration while identifying opportunities to strengthen service delivery and intern support.

Jack Townsend

Engagement & Resource Supervisor

Jack leads student engagement and communication initiatives that help students discover and utilize our services. He oversees resources, community engagement efforts, branding, and student feedback programs to ensure our outreach remains effective and impactful.

Peer Leadership Support

Senior Peer Navigators

Senior Peer Navigators are experienced, high-performing interns who take on additional responsibilities to help support the work of Supervisors and Managers while continuing to provide student support through appointments, live events, and other Peer Navigator services.

Support Other Interns

Provide peer leadership, encouragement, and practical support that helps other interns succeed in their roles.

Take on Additional Responsibilities

Assist with projects, processes, communication, and other work that supports the needs of the Peer Navigator program.

Continue Supporting Students

Remain active in student-facing work, balancing added leadership responsibilities with continued Peer Navigator service.

Senior Peer Navigators demonstrate strong organization, adaptability, and WGU Leadership Principles such as Integrity, Courage, and Urgency.
